Woovin Payouts

Drop one or more Woovin "Payout invoice" PDFs here. Every row is checked against Airtable, the Woovin API and Rompslomp first; nothing is booked until you press Book. Booking makes the sales invoices (with their Voorraadcorrectie), the credits, Woovin's fee expense, and one Kruisposten payment per document; the PDF itself is kept here, with its payout. Several at once are put in order and booked one after the other, oldest first.
