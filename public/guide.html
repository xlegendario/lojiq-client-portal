Lojiq · Client portal

How the portal works

Everything you can do from your account, in the order you will meet it. Sections that do not apply to your store are left out, so what you see below is your portal and nothing else.

01Signing in

Your account

We set your account up for you, so there is nothing to register. Sign in with the e-mail address and password you were given and you are in.

Want a password of your own? Use Forgot password? on the login screen and you will get a reset link by e-mail. You can do that whenever you like, not only when you have lost it.

One account per store. Everyone at your store works from the same login, so what one of you accepts, the others see.

02The shop

The shop is where you buy. Search a model, pick a size, and you see one price per pair — what it costs you, all in. No bidding, no waiting.

Buying a pair

Find the pair, choose the size, and press Buy. That settles it at the price on screen. The order appears under Manual Orders straight away and we take it from there.

Making an offer instead

Not happy with the price? Press Offer and name yours. We take it to our sellers, and you get an answer back in your offer-requests channel on Discord.

Asking for something we do not have

If a pair is not in the shop, place a Want To Buy. You give the model, the size, and the most you are willing to pay. That maximum is a ceiling, not a promise — you still decide when an offer comes back.

A Max Price is optional. Leave it empty and you simply see whatever comes in, with no limit set in advance.

Prices are shown in whole euros. What the shop quotes is what you are invoiced — no cents appearing later, and no fee on top.

03Manual Orders

Everything you bought or asked for yourself lives here, and each tab is a stage. A deal moves down the list from top to bottom.

Answering an offer

An offer reaches you in two places at once: in the Offers tab here, and as a message in your offer-requests channel on Discord. They are the same offer — answer it wherever suits you and the other updates itself.

  • Accept — the price is agreed and the deal starts.
  • Counter — name your own number. It goes back to the seller, who can accept, counter again, or walk away.
  • Deny — you pass. Your want-to-buy stays live and the next seller can still offer.

Denying does not close anything. It only tells that one seller no.

An offer expires when a better one arrives. If a second seller comes in lower, the older message greys out and the new one takes its place. Always answer the live one.

04Store Orders

These arrive on their own. When a customer orders from your webshop and you cannot supply it yourself, it lands here and we source it for you. The stages read the same as Manual Orders, with one extra tab.

Issues

Anything that needs a decision from you — a pair that could not be sourced, a delivery that went wrong, a return that will not close. If this tab has a number on it, something is genuinely waiting.

05Returns

A return starts here and stays here until it is closed. Register it, send the pair back, and follow it through the same way you follow an order.

Register before you ship. A pair arriving without a registered return cannot be matched to anything, which is what turns a two-day return into a two-week one.

06StockX

Your dedicated StockX account, as far as it touches your orders here. Only relevant if your store has a dedicated StockX account.

07Finance

Everything you owe sits under Open Payments. You are not billed per pair — you select what you want to settle and pay it in one go.

Paying

Tick the rows you want to pay, press the pay button, and you get one payment link for the lot. Once it clears, those rows move on by themselves.

Only rows that are actually payable can be selected. A pair still being sourced is not billed yet.

08Discord

The buttons in Discord do the real thing. Accepting an offer there is the same as accepting it in the portal — there is no second confirmation to find afterwards.

Something not covered here? Ask us in your own channel — that is what it is for.
