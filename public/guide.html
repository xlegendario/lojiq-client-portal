Client portal

How the portal works

Everything you need to manage sourcing, orders, shipping and payments from one place. Your setup may include Store Orders, Manual Orders, or both — the line below shows which order flow is active for your store.

01Signing in

Your account

We create your account for you, so there is nothing to register. Sign in with the e-mail address and password you received to access the portal.

Want to set or change your password? Use Forgot password? on the login screen and we will send you a reset link by e-mail.

Your store uses one shared account, so actions and updates stay visible to everyone working from the same login.

02Your dashboard

This is your main workspace. Orders, sourcing activity, shipping and payments are all managed from here, with each section showing where a deal currently stands.

The menu on the left

Each section opens into the stages an order can move through. The number beside a stage shows how many orders are currently there, giving you a quick overview of what is happening across your account.

The sections available to you depend on your store setup, so your menu may look slightly different from the examples in this guide.

The four counters at the top

Current View shows how many orders are in the table you are currently viewing. Open Orders, Shipped and Fulfilled show totals across your full account, regardless of which stage is open.

The table

Each row represents one pair, with the available actions shown on the right. Some stages include extra filters — for example, Offers can be filtered by Open, Countered and Denied.

The number beside the title shows how many rows are currently in that view. Use the arrow next to it to refresh the table without reloading the full page.

Search

Search within the stage you are viewing by order number, product, SKU, size or tracking number. If you cannot find an order, first check the stage you would expect it to be in.

03The shop

The Shop is where you source pairs for yourself. Browse the inventory available through our network, choose a size, and either buy at the available price or make an offer. If the pair is not listed, you can post a Want To Buy instead.

Finding a pair

Search by SKU or product name, then narrow the results by brand or size. You can also sort by price or product name. The line below the toolbar shows how many products match your current filters.

Each product card shows the lowest available price for that model and how many sizes are currently available. Open a product to see the price and availability for each size.

Stock type

Use the three stock-type buttons to choose which inventory can be shown. Your selection also determines how prices are displayed.

SettingWhat you get, and how the price reads
All stockAll inventory available to your store. Prices are shown including VAT.
B2B onlyOnly inventory that can be invoiced with VAT. Prices are shown excluding VAT, and a valid VAT ID is required. For stores based in the Netherlands, 21% VAT is added to the invoice and can be reclaimed. For stores outside the Netherlands, VAT is reverse-charged, so no VAT is added to the invoice and you account for it in your own country.
Margin onlyOnly margin-scheme inventory. Prices are shown including VAT, with no VAT shown separately on the invoice.

The same setting also applies when you post a Want To Buy. Your Max Price is interpreted according to the stock type selected at the time you place it.

Buying a pair

Find the pair, choose the size and press Buy to place the order at the price shown. It will immediately appear under Manual Orders, where you can follow the rest of the process.

The seller holding the pair is asked to confirm availability. At the same time, the request is automatically sent to other eligible sources at that price and posted as a Want To Buy across our seller network, giving the order multiple ways to be fulfilled from the moment you place it.

Making an offer instead

Want a better price? Press Offer and submit the amount you are willing to pay. Your offer is sent into our seller network, and any response appears in both the portal and your offer-requests channel on Discord.

Asking for something we do not have

If you cannot find the pair you need, post a Want To Buy. Select the model and size, then optionally set the maximum price you are willing to consider. Sellers across our network can then compete to fulfill it.

A Max Price is a ceiling, not a commitment. Leave it empty if you want to receive all offers and decide on them yourself.

Prices are shown in whole euros. The price shown in the Shop is the amount used for the order — no additional sourcing fee is added afterwards.

04Manual Orders

Manual Orders contains everything you source yourself through the Shop: direct Buys, Offers and Want To Buys. Each tab represents a stage in the process, so you can follow every request from sourcing to delivery.

TabWhat it means
Open OrdersYour active Want To Buys and sourcing requests that are still waiting for a deal.
OffersA seller has made an offer. Accept it, counter it or deny it.
ProcessingA deal has been agreed and the pair is being processed. No action is needed from you here.
AllocatedThe pair has been secured and allocated to your order. The supplier can now prepare it for shipping.
Label RequestsThe supplier is ready to ship and has requested a label. Upload it here.
Ready To ShipThe shipping label has been provided and the pair is ready to ship.
ShippedThe pair is on its way. Tracking is available from the order.
FulfilledThe order has been delivered and the process is complete.

Answering an offer

Seller offers appear in two places at the same time: under Offers in the portal and in your offer-requests channel on Discord. Both are connected, so responding in either place updates the same negotiation.

  • Accept — accept the seller's price and move the deal forward.
  • Counter — send back the price you are willing to pay.
  • Deny — decline the current offer. Your request stays live for other sellers.

Denying an offer does not close your request. The seller is notified and encouraged to try again with a better price, while your request stays open to the rest of the network. So even if the price doesn't work for you, denying is always better than leaving the offer unanswered.

One negotiation, multiple competing sellers. Every new seller offer has to beat the Current Lowest. When you send a counter, it reaches all sellers still competing for the request at the same time. They can accept it or come back with a better price, while any new seller joining has to beat the best offer already on the table.

You only manage the latest action from your dashboard instead of negotiating separately in multiple DMs. One negotiation for you, competition between the sellers behind it.
Always respond to the current offer. When a better seller offer comes in, the previous one becomes inactive and is shown in grey. The newest live offer is the one that can be acted on.

05Store Orders

Store Orders are created automatically through your webshop integration. When an eligible customer order needs sourcing, it enters Lojiq through the API and our network can start working on it without you having to create the request manually.

TabWhat it means
Open OrdersStore orders that are still waiting to be sourced or matched with an offer.
OffersA seller has made an offer. Accept it, counter it or deny it.
ProcessingA deal has been agreed and the pair is being processed. No action is needed from you here.

Label RequestsThe supplier is ready to ship and has requested a label. Upload it here.
Ready To ShipThe shipping label has been provided and the pair is ready to ship.
ShippedThe pair is on its way. Tracking is available from the order.
FulfilledThe order has been delivered and the process is complete.
IssuesAn order needs your attention or a decision. This tab only exists for Store Orders.

Answering an offer

Offers work the same way as they do for Manual Orders. A seller offer appears under Offers and in your offer-requests channel on Discord at the same time. Respond in either place and the negotiation updates everywhere.

  • Accept — accept the seller's price and move the deal forward.
  • Counter — send back the price you are willing to pay.
  • Deny — decline the current offer. The Store Order stays open for other sellers.

The difference is that a Store Order already belongs to a customer order from your webshop, so keeping these moving helps you fulfill your customer as quickly as possible.

The same centralized negotiation works here. Sellers compete to fulfill your Store Order, every new offer has to beat the Current Lowest, and a counter from you reaches all sellers still competing at once. You manage one negotiation while the network competes behind it.

Issues

The Issues tab collects Store Orders that need your attention, such as a sourcing problem, delivery issue or another exception that cannot continue automatically. If the tab shows a number, there is something waiting for you to review.

06Returns

Use Returns to register and follow a return from the moment it is created until it is completed. Once registered, the return stays visible here while it moves through the process.

Always register the return before shipping it. This lets us match the incoming pair to the correct order as soon as it arrives and prevents unnecessary delays.

07Finance

Open Payments shows everything currently ready to be paid. Select multiple rows and settle them together instead of handling each pair as a separate payment.

Paying

Select the rows you want to settle and press the pay button. One payment link is created for the full selection, and the payment statuses update automatically once it has been completed.

Only orders that are ready for payment can be selected. Orders that are still being sourced will not appear as payable yet.

08Discord

Your store has its own Discord channels for the different types of order updates. The portal is your central overview, while Discord keeps your team updated as actions and status changes happen.

ChannelWhat arrives there
new-ordersNew Store Orders that enter Lojiq for your store.
offer-requestsSeller offers, counter offers and other negotiation updates that need your attention.
fulfillment-updatesUpdates when an order is allocated and the pair has been secured.
labelsShipping-label requests, including the link to upload the label.
shipment-updatesUpdates when an order has shipped.
shipment-delaysAlerts when an order has not shipped within its expected timeframe.
Discord actions are connected to the portal. Accepting or responding to an offer in Discord updates the same negotiation in your dashboard, so there is no need to repeat the action in both places.

Something not covered here? Ask us in your store channel and we will help you from there.
